A self-improving AI harness that creates architectural designs within building codes.

You write the intent, the programme and the site. Each round, ARCHLOOP generates the design, checks it against building rules that cite their source, and revises it. You stay the author and steer by critique.

Simple as 1-2-3-4

Design in rounds, like architects already do

Architects work from an idea, through rounds of design and critique. ARCHLOOP follows that process instead of asking you to bend to drafting software.

1

Write the brief

Intent, programme, site and ideas, in plain text, in your own language.

2

Generate

The harness builds a design from the brief, one option per idea you want to compare.

3

Check

Building-code, geometry and brief rules run after every round. Each rule names its source.

4

Revise

Failed checks are fixed and the round runs again. Your critique sets the next round.

What works, and what comes next

ARCHLOOP is an early venture built on an open-source core.

Works today

  • Brief to massing, rules after every round, a bounded revision loop
  • German building-code and accessibility checks, each with its source
  • Options compared side by side, exported to IFC4 and OpenUSD
  • Runs on your own computer; your project data stays with you

Next

  • AI agents generating the layout in each round
  • Exact geometry and floor layouts
  • Zoning figures (GRZ, GFZ) and state building codes
  • Learning from every project, and a report you can hand to a client

Open

Built on arch-harness, an open-source core (Apache-2.0) your office can read and adapt.

Traceable

Every check cites the rule behind it, so a client or a building authority can follow a decision.

Yours

Open formats and a loop that runs locally: no lock-in, no upload of client data.
