The problem

Architects spend their time on lines, not ideas

Every change means redrawing

Move one wall, and plans, sections and areas follow by hand.

Code problems show up late

Accessibility and setbacks are checked after the design is fixed.

Few options get tested

Fees and hours allow one or two variants, rarely the best one.
